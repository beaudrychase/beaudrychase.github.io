Currents are shifting in the world of personal computing. For years one of the foundational assumptions was that devices would get cheaper to produce over time.
A longstanding assumption for device manufacturers is inverted. Memory and storage hardware costs are rising, and with current trends it looks like compute costs are on their way up (source). 
Devices that have been on the market for years have seen price increases.
